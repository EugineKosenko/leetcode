Create two separate lists for closed and open boxes. Open boxes form a processing queue.
When creating the lists, we distribute the initial boxes.

#+begin_src rust :noweb-ref task1298:Actions
  let (mut closed, mut opened) = initial_boxes.into_iter()

          (closed, opened)

#+end_src
